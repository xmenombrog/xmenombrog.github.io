Overview

The game

Two players share one keyboard, each piloting a spaceship in the same arena. The match runs until one ship is destroyed.

  • Local two-player battles on a single keyboard
  • Ship movement and shooting, with simple physics for movement and collisions
  • A health bar for each player that drains with every hit
  • A game-over screen that names the winner or calls a tie, with Restart and Quit
  • Background music, a blaster sound effect, pixel-art textures and pixel fonts
  • Frame rate capped at 60 frames per second

Not in this version yet: a main menu and advanced gameplay options.

Under the hood

Written entirely in C++ on SFML’s graphics, window and audio modules, and organized into a class per game object.

Spacewar.Project/ C++, 100% of code
Game.hGame.cpp
The game itself: the window, keyboard input, sound, health bars and the game-over screen.
Spaceship.hSpaceship.cpp
A player’s ship, including its hit points.
Bullet.hBullet.cpp
The projectiles the ships fire.
Texture/Sound/Font/
Pixel-art background, ship, bullet and explosion textures; music and sound effects; pixel fonts.
