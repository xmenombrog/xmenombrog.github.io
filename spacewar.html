01 — Overview

The game

Two players share one keyboard, each piloting a spaceship in the same arena. The match runs until one ship is destroyed.

  • 01 Local two-player battles on a single keyboard
  • 02 Ship movement and shooting, with simple physics for movement and collisions
  • 03 A health bar for each player that drains with every hit
  • 04 A game-over screen that names the winner or calls a tie, with Restart and Quit
  • 05 Background music, a blaster sound effect, pixel-art textures and pixel fonts
  • 06 Frame rate capped at 60 frames per second

Not in this version yet: a main menu and advanced gameplay options.

03 — Code

Under the hood

Written entirely in C++ on SFML’s graphics, window and audio modules, and organized into a class per game object.
