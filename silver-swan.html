What the app says

    A screening demo with readings you set by hand, for illustration only. It is not a medical device and not medical advice.

    System

    How a sample is read

    A sealed optical chamber replaces room lighting and guesswork, and three measurements are read together, because color alone can misread hydration.

    • Controlled enclosure. An opaque housing with a matte-black interior and fixed white light, so ambient light never reaches the sample.
    • Multi-modal sensing. A digital pH sensor, a TDS sensor for specific gravity, an AS7341 11-channel light sensor and an ESP32 camera.
    • Offline apps. Readings travel over Bluetooth Low Energy to native apps that store and classify them on the phone, with no internet needed.
    • EF-KNN classification. Fuzzy memberships weight each neighbor’s vote, assigning one of eight hydration levels and raising flags A–C.

    Results

    What the validation showed

    Validated across 150 independent trials with chemically modified synthetic urine, spanning eight hydration levels and four abnormality classes.

    100% Hydration classification accuracy All eight levels correct, macro AUC-ROC 1.00
    7.72% pH sensor error (MAPE) Within the 10% threshold
    0.13% Specific gravity error (MAPE) Within the 10% threshold
    99.33% Bluetooth delivery rate Mean sensor-to-screen time about 8.6 s
